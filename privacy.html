Arcana Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Arcana Sort does not require an account and the current app build does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, rewards, and other gameplay state may be stored locally on your device so the game can resume and remember your preferences.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Arcana Sort does not receive your payment card details. The app may receive purchase status information from Apple so it can unlock purchased features or restore purchases.

Tracking

The current app build does not perform cross-app tracking and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Children

Arcana Sort is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

If future versions add services such as advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before those services are shipped.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the Arcana Sort support page.
