Arcana Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Arcana Sort does not require an Arcana Sort account. Core game progress, settings, rewards, earned currency, collection state, and other gameplay state are stored locally on the device so the game can resume and remember player preferences.

Advertising and consent

Arcana Sort includes Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements. Before requesting ads, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow where required. The app requests ads only when the Google consent SDK reports that ads may be requested. A privacy-options entry is available in the app when the consent SDK reports that privacy options are required.

Google Mobile Ads may process technical device, app, consent, and advertising-delivery information according to Google's policies and the choices available to the player. Advertising consent is separate from the Analytics setting described below.

Optional analytics

Build 13 includes Unity Analytics 6.3.0 for optional gameplay analytics. Analytics is off by default. Players can enable or disable Analytics in Settings, and existing players are not automatically opted in when the feature is added.

When Analytics is enabled, Arcana Sort may record gameplay and economy events such as level starts and completions, move counts, helper use, earned and spent in-game currency, purchase success or failure status, advertising presentation events, and collection or world reward progress.

Unity Analytics also processes SDK-level technical and usage information as described by Unity's privacy documentation and the privacy manifest shipped with the Unity Analytics SDK. Gameplay, saves, rewards, and purchases do not depend on analytics delivery; if Analytics is disabled or unavailable, the game continues normally.

Revoking Analytics in Settings stops optional analytics collection through the app's Unity Analytics integration. The integration also supports a provider data-deletion request. For assistance with a deletion request, use the support contact below.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit / Unity IAP. Arcana Sort does not receive the user's full payment card information. The app may receive verified purchase status and transaction information from Apple so it can unlock purchased features, prevent duplicate fulfillment, and restore purchases.

Data stored on your device

Local data can include level progress, game settings, earned rewards, purchase-entitlement state, and other gameplay state. This data remains on the device until the app data is removed or the application is uninstalled, subject to platform backup behavior.

Tracking and privacy choices

Advertising privacy choices and Analytics consent are separate controls. Analytics consent does not grant permission for cross-company tracking.

If a shipping advertising or analytics configuration performs tracking as Apple defines it or requires access to an identifier that needs App Tracking Transparency permission, Arcana Sort must request the applicable Apple permission before that tracking occurs. The App Store privacy disclosures are maintained to reflect the third-party SDKs included in the shipping build.

Children

Arcana Sort is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. The game does not require a user account or ask players to submit personal profile information.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit / Unity IAP — purchase processing and restoration
  • Google Mobile Ads / User Messaging Platform — advertising delivery and consent management
  • Unity Analytics 6.3.0 — optional gameplay analytics after explicit in-app opt-in

Changes

This policy will be updated before a release materially changes the app's data-processing, advertising, analytics, or purchase-provider configuration.

Support

For privacy, analytics-data deletion, or support questions, use the Arcana Sort support page.
