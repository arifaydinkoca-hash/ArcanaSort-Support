Arcana Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Arcana Sort does not require an account. Core game progress, settings, rewards, and other gameplay state are stored locally on the device so the game can resume and remember player preferences.

Advertising and consent

Arcana Sort includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements. TestFlight and other beta builds may use Google's official test ad units while advertising behavior is being validated.

Before requesting ads, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow where required. The app only requests ads when the Google consent SDK reports that ads may be requested. A privacy-options entry is available in the app when the consent SDK reports that privacy options are required.

Google Mobile Ads may process technical device, app, consent, and advertising-delivery information according to Google's own policies and the consent choices available to the player. Arcana Sort does not directly implement its own cross-app tracking logic.

Analytics

Arcana Sort does not currently send gameplay analytics events to a separate analytics provider. Gameplay analytics hooks in the current build use a local/no-op implementation.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit / Unity IAP. Arcana Sort does not receive the user's full payment card information. The app may receive verified purchase status and transaction information from Apple so it can unlock purchased features, prevent duplicate fulfillment, and restore purchases.

Data stored on your device

Local data can include level progress, game settings, earned rewards, purchase-entitlement state, and other gameplay state. This data remains on the device until the app data is removed or the application is uninstalled, subject to platform backup behavior.

Tracking and privacy choices

The current Build 13 beta does not itself implement an App Tracking Transparency request or directly access the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Advertising availability and consent choices are handled through the Google Mobile Ads / UMP privacy flow and iOS platform behavior. If the shipping configuration changes to use tracking as defined by Apple, this policy and the app's permission flow will be updated before that configuration is released.

Children

Arcana Sort is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. The game does not require a user account or ask players to submit personal profile information.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit / Unity IAP — purchase processing and restoration
  • Google Mobile Ads / User Messaging Platform — advertising delivery and consent management

Changes

This policy will be updated before a release adds a materially different data-processing service or changes the advertising or analytics configuration.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the Arcana Sort support page.
